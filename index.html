Our Journey

How Immortals Started

Founded on January 1, 2021 in Mangaluru to build fearless combat athletes and transform youth lives.

01

The Founding Batches

Immortals began at Urwastore with Batch 1 (6 students), followed rapidly by Batch 2 (4 students) and Batch 3 (20 students) in Urwastore and Kulshekar.

Jan 2021 · Urwastore & Kulshekar origins
02

Multi-Branch Expansion

Growing demand led to new academy centers in Padavinangady and the establishment of our main headquarters branch at Thokkottu (Kapikad).

Thokkottu (Main Branch) & Padavinangady
03

Institutional Impact

Expanded martial arts & self-defense programs across 15 educational institutions in Dakshina Kannada, training over 500+ students to date.

500+ Students Trained · 15+ Schools & Colleges
