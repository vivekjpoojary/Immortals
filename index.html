About Us

Our Story

Immortals was created to give Mangaluru a focused training environment where people of different ages and experience levels can become stronger, more disciplined and more confident.

From first-time beginners to competitive athletes, every member follows structured coaching designed around technique, conditioning and consistent progress. Our academy brings eight martial arts and fitness disciplines together across our main locations in Thokkottu and Urwa.

We believe martial arts is more than learning how to fight. It develops composure, resilience and respect — qualities that extend far beyond the training floor.
